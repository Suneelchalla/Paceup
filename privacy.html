Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PaceUp ("the App") is a browser-based running pacer. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS to track your running route, pace, and distance in real time. This data is processed entirely on your device. Your GPS data is never sent to any server, never stored in any cloud, and never shared with any third party. Route data exists only in your browser's memory during a run and is discarded when you close the App, unless you choose to export a GPX file (which is saved locally to your device).

Run History

Completed run summaries (distance, time, pace, splits) are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Full GPS tracks are not stored in history. This data never leaves your device. You can clear your history at any time from within the App.

Voice / Audio

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine for voice guidance. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

Map Tiles

The App loads map images from OpenStreetMap's tile servers to display the map during your run. Your device's IP address is visible to OpenStreetMap when loading these tiles. This is the only network request the App makes. See OpenStreetMap's privacy policy.

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or third-party scripts beyond Leaflet.js (the open-source map library). There are no cookies.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update date at the top will reflect the change.

Contact
